# Warranty

## Coverage

All products carry a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
Wear and tear, accidental damage, and damage from misuse are not covered.

## Claims

To file a claim you need the order number and at least two clear photos
showing the defect. Most claims are resolved within 5 business days. Approved
claims result in either a replacement, a repair, or a refund, at our option.

## Extended warranty

A 2-year extension can be added at checkout for an extra fee. The extension
covers the same scope as the standard warranty; it does not add accidental
damage protection.
