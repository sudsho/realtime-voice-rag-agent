# Product care

## Cleaning

Use a soft, dry microfiber cloth for the screen. For the body, slightly damp
cloth with mild detergent is fine. Do not use isopropyl alcohol on coated
surfaces; it can strip the oleophobic layer over time.

## Storage

Store between 0 and 35 degrees Celsius. Avoid direct sunlight for extended
periods. If storing the device for more than a month, charge to about 50
percent first and power it off.

## Battery

Lithium-ion battery health degrades faster at high temperatures and at the
extremes of state of charge. Keeping the unit between 20 and 80 percent
charge most of the time helps long-term capacity.
