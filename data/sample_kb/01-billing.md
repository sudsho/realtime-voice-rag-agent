# Billing FAQ

## When am I charged?

We bill on the same day of the month you signed up. If you upgraded mid-cycle,
the new plan price is prorated for the remainder of the current cycle and the
full new price applies starting the next cycle.

## Refund policy

We offer a full refund within 14 days of the original purchase if you have not
used more than 25 percent of your monthly quota. After 14 days we issue
prorated credit toward the next billing cycle, not a cash refund.

## Failed payment

If a payment fails we retry three times across seven days. During retries your
account stays active. If all retries fail, the account moves to a read-only
state for 14 days, then is suspended. You can reactivate any time by updating
your payment method.

## Updating card details

Visit Settings > Billing > Payment method. Cards are validated with a 1 USD
authorization that is reversed within a few hours. The reversal can take up
to seven business days to clear depending on your bank.
