# Account management

## Resetting password

Go to the sign-in page and click "Forgot password". You will receive an email
with a reset link valid for 30 minutes. If the link expires, just request a
new one. We never include the new password in the email itself.

## Two-factor authentication

We support TOTP via any standard authenticator app. SMS codes are not
recommended and are being phased out by end of 2025. Backup codes are
generated when you first enable 2FA; store them somewhere offline.

## Closing your account

Closure is self-service from Settings > Account > Close account. Your data is
retained for 30 days in case you change your mind. After 30 days the data is
permanently deleted from primary storage. Backup retention is 90 days.

## Changing the account email

Email changes require a verification link sent to both the old and the new
address. If you no longer have access to the old address, contact support
with proof of ownership (last invoice, account ID).
