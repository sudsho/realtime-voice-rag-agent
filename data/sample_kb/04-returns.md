# Returns and exchanges

## Window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Some categories have
shorter windows: electronics 14 days, perishables not returnable, custom items
final sale.

## Process

Start a return from your order page. Print the prepaid label and drop the
package at any carrier location. Refund posts within 5 business days of
warehouse receipt.

## Exchanges

Exchanges are processed as a return plus a new order. This avoids stock
mismatches. We do not charge a second shipping fee on the new order if the
original return ships within 7 days of the request.
