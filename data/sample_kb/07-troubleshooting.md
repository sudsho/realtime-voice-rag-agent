# Troubleshooting

## Device will not power on

1. Connect the original charger and let it sit for 10 minutes. A deeply
   discharged battery does not respond to power-on for the first few minutes.
2. Try a different cable and outlet. Most "won't turn on" cases are bad
   cables or USB ports.
3. Hold the power button for 15 seconds to force restart.
4. If still nothing, contact support with the serial number.

## Connectivity issues

If the device cannot connect to your network, first verify other devices on
the same network can. Then forget the network on the device and re-add it.
For 5 GHz networks, ensure the channel is one of the standard 36, 40, 44, 48,
149, 153, 157, 161; some non-standard channels are not supported.

## Slow performance

A factory reset clears 90 percent of soft slowdowns. Back up first. After
reset, sign in and let the device sync for a few hours before reinstalling
heavy applications.
