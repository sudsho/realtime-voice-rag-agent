# Shipping

## Delivery times

Orders ship from the warehouse within 1 business day. Standard shipping in the
US is 3 to 5 business days. Expedited is 2 business days. International is
7 to 14 business days depending on customs.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed once your package leaves the warehouse. The carrier
takes 24 to 48 hours to scan it in, so the tracking page may not show updates
right away.

## Lost packages

If tracking shows delivered but you have not received the package, wait 48
hours (carriers sometimes mark in advance) and check with neighbors and the
mailroom. After that, contact us and we will open a carrier investigation.
Most resolved cases get a free replacement within 7 to 10 days.

## Address changes

If your order has not yet shipped, you can change the address from your order
detail page. Once it ships, the address is locked.
